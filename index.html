No. 03 / Find Us

Just off Wyresdale Road.

Lancaster Leisure Park, near Williamson's Park. Bus 18 from Lancaster Bus Station stops at the entrance.

Alchemy sits inside the expanding Lancaster Leisure Park, just off Wyresdale Road, a few minutes' walk from Williamson's Park. There's a free car park on site — all‑day parking, no ticket, no meter, no worry about your appointment overrunning.

If you're coming in from town, bus number 18 from Lancaster Bus Station stops right outside the park's entrance.

Same‑day appointments are often available — give us a ring and we'll see what we can fit in. For colour, please remember the 48‑hour patch test.
